# Provider model marks

These marks identify the model family answering a chat. They are not Rotli marks,
and their presence does not imply sponsorship or endorsement.

- `claude-spark-clay.svg`: Anthropic's official Claude Spark — Clay SVG from the
  maintainer-provided Anthropic media resources.
- `openai-blossom.svg`: OpenAI's official Blossom — Black SVG from the
  maintainer-provided OpenAI logo kit; `openai-blossom-white.svg` is the same
  artwork in OpenAI's white. OpenAI publishes the Blossom only in black and
  white, so the app shows black in light environments and white in dark ones.
  Its viewBox is cropped to the artwork for the shared 14px sidebar footprint.
- `gemma.svg`: Google DeepMind's official Gemma repository icon at commit
  `7b785991bd78626c73b317eb43fdbb6c292f7b9c`; a one-unit matching gradient
  stroke preserves its fine geometry at the shared 14px sidebar footprint.
- `gemini.svg` and `qwen.svg`: LobeHub Lobe Icons compact color vectors at
  commit `f07e9be35aef452ce735f95ea8204a14ecc513f7`, used under the included MIT
  license.
- `meta.svg`, `microsoft.svg`, and `mistral.svg`: LobeHub Lobe Icons compact
  color vectors (`meta-color`, `microsoft-color`, `mistral-color`) from
  `@lobehub/icons-static-svg` 1.91.0, used under the included MIT license to
  identify Llama, Phi, and Mistral-family local models.

Every mark renders as its own file in its vendor's colors — never tinted with
Rotli's theme ink, in every environment, on selected rows, and with Neutral
sidebar icons.

Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, Gemma, Qwen, Meta, Microsoft, and Mistral names and
marks belong to their respective owners. Use these files only to identify the
selected model family.
