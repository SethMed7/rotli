   Keep “One folder. / Plain files. / Room to think.” The ladder moves naturally from structure to material to benefit. Ten seconds, however, is almost a quarter of the film without product.

   - **0.0–1.6:** “One folder.”
   - **1.6–3.2:** Add “Plain files.”
   - **3.2–6.0:** Add “Room to think.”

   **Cut the Results beat entirely.** “Check, choose, decide” is vague, and True/Draw/False introduces a small syntax lesson when the audience still needs the product’s main idea.

   **Cut the search beat.** “⌘K finds anything in the folder” is an absolute claim unsupported by the supplied facts. A search-field crop would not demonstrate it anyway.

   Cut the sidebar beat as currently paired. The navigation labels do not show notes linking to one another, and a Tasks label alone does not establish aggregation behavior.

   Replace “Save a lesson to your files only when you want to.” That specific save behavior is not established in the public facts. “Ten practice lessons in the Playground” is concrete and supported.

4. **Design for reading time, not just whether captions fit.**

   The first two product captions contain 15–16 words in roughly 3.5 seconds. The first chat caption has 15 words in 2.2 seconds. Viewers also need to inspect unfamiliar UI; those timings are too tight.

   Keep captions to one short sentence where possible, two deliberately broken lines otherwise. At 1080p, 40 px is a reasonable starting point, but a large cocoa pill under every shot risks looking like a social-ad template. Prefer a **fixed linen caption area below the app**, with cocoa type and consistent placement.

   Keep caption text stationary through picture transitions. Do not crossfade outgoing and incoming sentences over each other. Burn captions into the export so muted playback works without depending on a player’s subtitle settings.

5. **Replace constant drift with purposeful reframing and genuine holds.**

   “So nothing freezes” is the wrong rule for a calm workspace. Stillness gives people time to understand the interface.

   At 1640 px wide, the 3840 px capture is displayed at approximately 43% of its original size. A 1.75× detail crop brings it to approximately 75%; a 2.4× crop is near native pixel scale. **Sharpness is probably adequate; text size and lost context are the bigger risks.**

   Use one 0.6–0.8-second reframe, then hold still for at least 3 seconds. Keep a recognizable landmark—sidebar edge, note title, or lesson heading—in each detail shot.

   The flat border is fine. Cut the decorative radius if it clips actual app chrome or makes the screenshot resemble an invented device frame. Retain real application proportions.

   Crucially, the Playground is a static capture: **no task “lands” or changes state.** Remove the click at 18.2. A timed success sound over an already-completed task implies an interaction the film has not shown.
