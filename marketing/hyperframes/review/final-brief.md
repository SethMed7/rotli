# rotli launch promo — final cut, read-only creative critique

You are reviewing the re-cut of a 42.8-second product film for a calm Mac notes
app. Critique it as a senior creative director and editor. **Read-only: do not
run tools, browse, read files, or write files.** Everything you need is here.
Reply in Markdown with concrete, numbered recommendations, most important
first. Say what to keep. Flag anything that reads as slideshow, generic
startup template, dishonest, or hard to read at a glance. Be specific about
timings and wording. Assume the film is watched muted on a laptop first.

## Public facts you may rely on

- rotli (lowercase) is a calm, local-first Mac workspace. One ordinary folder
  is the vault; notes are plain Markdown files the user keeps.
- Public launch focus: notes and chat. Writing, tasks, links, optional
  context-aware chat, six theme families (Rotli, Paper & Charcoal, Ocean,
  Grove, Iris, Midnight; each tuned light and dark), an app-owned Playground
  with ten practice lessons, "Save lesson to vault" as a real control.
- Chat is optional: an on-device model or a connected tool the user already
  trusts. Connected chat can send selected context, so the film must never say
  "never leaves your Mac". No chat transcript exists; the Chat beat is real
  navigation to the Chat front, not a model response.
- Do not market: Breve, DOCX/spreadsheet fidelity, relays, image generation,
  downloads. No "Download now". Status line: "Mac beta in preparation".
  Tagline: "Room to think. Files you keep."
- Brand: General Sans, Baloo 2 wordmark, linen #F8F2E9, cocoa #3A3028, clay
  #C97E62, dark ground #241D18 / linen text #F1E7DA (real dark-theme tokens).
  Flat material, no shadows or glows. Canonical quokka drawings exist.
- Footage: six synthetic app captures (same demo note in six themes), and one
  continuous synthetic browser recording of the Playground lesson "Tasks and
  progress": a real checkbox tick, a real Raw Markdown toggle (showing
  `- [x] Write the first draft`), toggling back, a real "Save lesson to vault"
  press (button becomes "Lesson saved", status "Your copy is in Main"), and a
  real press on the sidebar's Home/Chat switcher (sidebar shows New chat, All
  chats, model usage). Screenshots of those exact states extend holds.
- Output: 1920×1080, 30 fps, captions + original music + SFX only, no
  narration. Captions are burned into a linen band under the app frame,
  44 px General Sans, one line each; a VTT track duplicates them.
