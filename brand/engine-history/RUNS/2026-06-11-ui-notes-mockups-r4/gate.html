C · Inbox — what it actually is, and why local-first still holds

Round-3 confusion, answered head-on before any more Inbox UI.

Inbox is your existing email, not a new way to talk

Who you're talking to: the same people you already email. Inbox is a calm client for the accounts you connect — Gmail, iCloud, Fastmail, any IMAP. rotli never creates a messaging network, accounts don't talk through rotli, and there is no rotli server in the path. Replying sends a normal email through your own account.

Where conversations are saved: they already live on your mail provider (that's what email is). rotli keeps a local cache of headers + bodies in .rotli/ (SQLite) so reading, search, and thread-linked notes work instantly and offline. Delete rotli and your mail is untouched on the server; your linked notes stay as plain .md.

Why this fits local-first v1: it was always the one stated exception — “everything works offline except fetching new email.” The only network rotli ever touches is the provider you connect, opt-in per account. And to be precise about sequence: v1 ships Notes only — Inbox is the Phase-5 module; round 3's frame was a chassis proof, not a v1 commitment.
