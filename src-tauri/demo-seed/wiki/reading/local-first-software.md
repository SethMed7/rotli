# Local-first software

A reading note on the idea behind apps like rotli: software where your data lives
on your own device first, and the cloud is a convenience — not the owner.

The pitch, in short:

- **No spinners.** Your data is local, so the app is instant.
- **Works offline.** A dropped connection is a non-event.
- **You keep it.** If the company disappears, your files don't.
- **Private by default.** Your notes never have to leave the machine.

The trade-off is sync — keeping devices in agreement without a central server is
the hard part. Worth it: an app you can trust with the things you actually think.
