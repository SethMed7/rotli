# Welcome to rotli

rotli is a warm, local-first notes app that lives in your menu bar. Summon it,
write, and let it slip away — your thoughts stay in plain markdown files you own,
in one folder on your Mac.

You're reading this note in **Main** — your hand-arranged shelf. The very same
note also lives in the **Library**, filed under *guides*. One file, two ways in.

## Try a few things

- [ ] Press your **Open** shortcut to summon and hide the window from anywhere
- [ ] Hit **Quick capture** to drop a thought into the Inbox without leaving what you're doing
- [ ] Star a note to pin it to Quick access
- [ ] Ask the built-in chat a question — it can read across your notes

> Fast to capture, calm to return to. Everything here is a plain file you can open
> in any editor, forever.
