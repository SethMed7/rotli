# Main and the Brain

Every note you make starts in **Main** — the shelf you arrange by hand, in the
order that makes sense to you. Nothing about Main is automatic; it's your view.

Behind the scenes, a small on-device AI files each note into the **Brain** — the
areas like *guides*, *ideas*, and *reading* you see below. That's where the note
actually lives on disk.

The two aren't copies. They're the **same file**, reached two ways:

- **Main** is a tunnel — your arrangement, pointing at the file.
- **The Brain** is the home — where the file rests once it's filed.

Rearrange Main all you like; the Brain keeps things findable. Move a note in the
Brain; your Main arrangement stays put. Your order, and a tidy library, at once.
