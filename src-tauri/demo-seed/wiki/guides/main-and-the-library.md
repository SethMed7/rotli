# Main and the Library

Every note you make starts in **Main** — the shelf you arrange by hand, in the
order that makes sense to you. Nothing about Main is automatic; it's your view.

If you enable the on-device Librarian, it files each note into the **Library** —
the areas like *guides*, *ideas*, and *reading* you see below. That's where the
note actually lives on disk. A raw vault leaves the organization entirely to you.

The two aren't copies. They're the **same file**, reached two ways:

- **Main** is a tunnel — your arrangement, pointing at the file.
- **The Library** is the home — where the file rests once it's filed.

Rearrange Main all you like; the Library keeps things findable. Move a note in
the Library; your Main arrangement stays put. Your order, and a tidy library, at once.
